Quick Signup

Three steps to your first lesson.

  • 1

    Choose a program

    Pick an instrument, voice, or studio production track from the options above.

  • 2

    Pick a time

    We'll match you with an instructor and a slot that fits your week.

  • 3

    Confirm & pay

    Lock in your first lesson — co-op members get a standing discount.

Do you rent instruments?

Yes — guitars, keyboards, and student violins are available to rent for the term.

What ages do you teach?

From age 5 through adult, across all four programs listed above.

Can I switch instructors?

Yes, anytime — just ask your current instructor or reach out through the contact info below.

Is there a trial lesson?

New students can book a single trial lesson before committing to a term.
